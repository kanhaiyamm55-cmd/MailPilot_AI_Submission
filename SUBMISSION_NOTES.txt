
MAILPILOT AI
AI-POWERED EMAIL MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD

PROJECT OVERVIEW
MailPilot AI is a responsive email management dashboard prototype designed for personal assistants. It helps organize conversations, identify important emails, determine who should respond, and highlight messages that require personal client attention.

IMPLEMENTED FEATURES
1. Overview dashboard and email statistics.
2. Smart inbox with sample email conversations.
3. Search by sender, subject, and email content.
4. Filters for client reply, assistant reply, and awaiting response.
5. Client Attention section.
6. Email detail view with AI-style summary.
7. Response responsibility recommendation.
8. Suggested reply editing and copy functionality.
9. Add sample email interaction.
10. Add demo email account interaction.
11. Responsive mobile-friendly layout.

TECHNOLOGIES
HTML5
CSS3
Vanilla JavaScript

HOW TO TEST
1. Open the deployed website.
2. Navigate through Overview, Smart Inbox, Client Attention, and Email Accounts.
3. Search for an email.
4. Apply different inbox filters.
5. Open a conversation and inspect its AI insights.
6. Edit and copy the suggested reply.
7. Add a demo email and a demo account.
8. Test the layout on a mobile screen.

PROTOTYPE DISCLOSURE
This is a frontend demonstration prototype.
Email accounts and sample conversations are simulated.
AI classifications, summaries, and reply suggestions are predefined demonstration data.
Real email authentication, live AI API integration, database storage, and email sending are not implemented.
